**Favorite Series: ** *Queen of Tears*

Queen of Tears is a South Korean romantic drama about billionaire heiress Hong Hae-in (played by Kim Ji-won) and her small-town lawyer husband Baek Hyun-woo (played by Kim Soo-hyun), whose marriage is on the brink of divorce until she receives a terminal brain tumor diagnosis. Facing her looming death and a ruthless corporate takeover by manipulative rival Yoon Eun-sung (played by Park Sung-hoon), the estranged couple is forced to set aside their pride, fight back together, and rediscover the love they lost.
